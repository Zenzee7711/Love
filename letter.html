My Dearest Love,

It still feels a little unbelievable that everything started from a simple message on a screen. Out of all the people in this world, we found each other online. At first, it was just casual talking, small conversations, late-night chats, random jokes. I didn't expect anything special. But slowly, you became the person I looked forward to every single day.

I remember how excited and nervous I felt before we met in person for the first time. And then the moment I saw you, it felt even better. Your smile was real, your laugh was warmer, and the connection we built through messages suddenly became something I could see and feel.

That first meeting wasn't just a meeting at a cafe. It was the moment our story truly began. From typing "good morning" to hearing your voice.

And now, here we are celebrating 1 year and 8 months together. A whole year and a half of memories, growth, laughter, understanding, and love. Twenty months may sound like just a number, but to me, it represents every moment we chose each other, every challenge we faced together, and every day we continued building something real.
